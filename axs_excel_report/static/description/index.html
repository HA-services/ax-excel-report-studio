The spreadsheet your customer asked for, straight from Odoo

An export gives raw columns. Your accountant, your warehouse and your customers want a document: a header, the lines, totals that add up, sometimes product pictures, and the layout they already know. Excel Report Studio fills that document from Odoo. Build it in a designer, or design it in Excel and type placeholders such as {{ object.partner_id.name }} where the values belong. The report then sits in the Print menu, next to your PDF reports, for the users allowed to print it.

No-code designer

Pick header fields, a table of lines and totals. The styled workbook with your logo is built for you.

Your own Excel files

Upload any .xlsx with placeholders. Colors, merged cells, formulas, charts and print settings are kept.

Lines, loops and totals

Repeat a row or a block of rows per line or per record. Formulas, charts and formats grow with the lines.

Forms and lists

Print one record or a selection: one sheet per record, one file per record in a zip, or one workbook.

Download, attach, email

An optional dialog previews the result and attaches the file to the record or opens the email composer.

Companies and groups

Offer each report to some companies or user groups only. Printing follows the user's access rights.

Designer reports

Choose the model, the fields and the columns. Related fields, totals, title, footer and accent color included.

Your layout, your formulas

Type {{ for line in object.order_line }} in the first cell of a row and it repeats for every line. =SUM(F12:F12) below it becomes the sum of all lines, and the product picture of each line lands in its own row.

Charts that follow the data

A chart over the repeated row shows every printed record. Conditional formats, validations, filters and print areas follow too.

Questions

Do I need Excel on the server?
No. Files are produced by the module and open in Excel, LibreOffice, Google Sheets or Numbers.

Are numbers exported as text?
No. Numbers, dates and amounts are real values with their format, ready for formulas.

Does it work with custom models?
Yes. Any model can have Excel reports, including models added by other modules.

How many records can I print at once?
Up to 5,000 in one report.
